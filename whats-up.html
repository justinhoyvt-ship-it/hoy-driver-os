What's Up
in Vermont

Vermont's calendar moves with the seasons. These categories describe the kinds of activity and events that shape demand, travel patterns, and local movement across the region.

This page describes event categories, not a specific schedule. Check local event calendars and venue listings for current dates and details.
01 / Outdoor & Recreation

Trails, peaks, and water

Vermont's outdoor economy runs year-round. Hiking season peaks through summer and fall. Ski and snowboard activity dominates the winter corridor from Stowe and Sugarbush to Mad River Glen.

  • Hiking: Green Mountain trails, Camel's Hump, Mount Mansfield
  • Skiing and snowboarding: Stowe, Sugarbush, Bolton Valley, Mad River Glen
  • Cycling and bikepacking: Kingdom Trails, VAST trail system
  • Lake Champlain: kayaking, sailing, paddleboard, fishing
  • Running and trail races: local 5Ks to ultras across the region
02 / Arts & Culture

Galleries, theater, and museums

Burlington and the surrounding region support a steady arts scene, from major museum collections to community galleries and independent theater throughout the year.

  • ECHO Leahy Center: science and Lake Champlain exhibits
  • Fleming Museum of Art at UVM
  • Vermont Arts Council exhibitions and touring shows
  • FlynnSpace and Flynn Center performances
  • Independent galleries along Church Street and the South End Arts District
03 / Music & Live Entertainment

Venues, clubs, and festivals

Live music is a constant in Burlington. Clubs, bars, and outdoor spaces host local and touring acts throughout the year, with festivals drawing regional audiences in summer and fall.

  • Higher Ground: Burlington's primary live music venue
  • Nectar's, Club Metronome, and waterfront venues
  • Vermont Jazz Center performances
  • Summer outdoor concert series at parks and City Hall Park
  • Fall foliage festivals with music programming
04 / Food, Farms & Markets

Local food and agricultural culture

Vermont's food identity is woven into the region's calendar. Farmers markets, farm dinners, harvest events, and local brewery culture generate consistent activity across the seasons.

  • Burlington Farmers Market (City Hall Park, seasonal)
  • Intervale Community Farm and farm tours
  • Craft brewery and cidery taprooms across Chittenden County
  • Vermont Cheese Trail and dairy farm visits
  • Fall harvest events, cider pressing, and apple season
05 / Community & Local Events

Fairs, festivals, and town life

Vermont's community calendar is active through every season — from First Night Burlington to town meetings, block parties, and regional fairs that anchor the area's civic life.

  • First Night Burlington (New Year's Eve)
  • Pride march and community gatherings
  • Champlain Valley Fair (late summer, Essex Junction)
  • Local town fairs and grange events
  • Neighborhood festivals and waterfront events
06 / College & University Activity

UVM and Burlington's student calendar

The University of Vermont anchors a significant share of Burlington's year-round activity. Move-in, graduation, and athletic seasons each generate notable travel and demand patterns.

  • UVM move-in and orientation (late August)
  • Commencement weekend (mid-May)
  • UVM men's and women's hockey (fall through March)
  • Campus lectures, conferences, and performances
  • Champlain College and Saint Michael's College events

How this connects to the Pulse project

These categories inform the local-signal layer of the Pulse driver cockpit — not as a public schedule, but as context that helps a Vermont driver read what kind of movement is likely in a given window. The cockpit is private and driver-only. This page describes the categories it draws from.
