Site notes

What these pages do — and do not do.

What's Up in Vermont

This destination and community resource links visitors to public events, markets, destinations, and third-party planning sites. Dates, availability, admission, routes, and policies should always be confirmed with the original source.

Pulse interest

Submitting interest does not reserve, guarantee, price, or dispatch a ride. Pulse responds directly when appropriate.

Authorized requests

A code or QR allows access to a private request workflow after direct contact. A submitted request still requires a response and confirmation.

Experienced gig drivers

References to experienced gig drivers describe driving experience and local context. The public site does not publish a driver marketplace or universal availability.

Paid placement

Sponsored events, destination spotlights, and partner logo placements will be clearly labeled. Payment will not secretly determine organic ranking.

External services

Third-party calendars, maps, transportation providers, tourism sites, and payment tools maintain their own terms. Links do not imply endorsement or control.
