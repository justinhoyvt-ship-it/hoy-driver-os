Site notes

Pulse Vermont is a local independent project in testing.

Interest list

Submitting the form adds your email to a project-testing list. It does not request, schedule, reserve, or purchase transportation.

Current scope

The public site explains the driver cockpit, local signal work, and the early cooperative idea. Public ride requests and payments are not active.

Changes

The design, features, testing status, and direction may change as the project develops.
