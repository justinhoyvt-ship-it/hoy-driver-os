How it thinks

Five inputs. One score. Nothing hidden.

Flight

BTV arrivals, clustered near your window and weighted by delay.

Event

Local shows and venues, time-windowed so a 9pm set doesn't run the whole day.

Weather

Sudden precipitation and wind shifts, tracked in 15-minute resolution.

History

Your own logged $/hour by zone — not a citywide average.

Baseline

A fallback pattern, used only when nothing else has enough signal — and labeled as exactly that.

Every score traces back to these five. No black box.
