Privacy

Keep the public side public — and the ride side private.

Public browsing

What's Up in Vermont links to public destination and event resources. Pulse does not need your location to browse these pages.

Interest form

The public form collects only the details you choose to submit so Pulse can respond. It is not a booking.

Authorized rides

Ride requests, contact details, and confirmed status belong in the separate authorized workflow.

Location information

The public site does not request geolocation. A future confirmed-ride status tool may display the driver's progress toward pickup after explicit authorization. Driver location sharing should run only for the confirmed trip, pause when the driver page is hidden or offline, and expire after completion.

Third-party sources

External event, tourism, mapping, payment, and destination links are governed by their own privacy policies. Pulse does not control those sites.

Contact and correction

To ask about information you submitted, request correction or removal, or report an issue, email justinhoyvt@gmail.com.
